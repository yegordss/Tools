Thread Extractor

Pulls a clean copy of a conversation thread out of the page — clones it, strips the request-list panel, hidden nodes, icons, and the collapse / back / more-actions controls — then opens the result in its own tab.

📎 Extract Thread Drag this to your bookmarks bar — no install, nothing sent anywhere.
  1. Show your bookmarks bar (Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + B) if it isn't already visible.
  2. Drag the 📎 Extract Thread button above onto the bar.
  3. On the page with the thread you want, click the bookmark. A new tab opens with just the cleaned-up thread.
